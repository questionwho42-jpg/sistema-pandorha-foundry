# 👥 Guia de Prompts: NPCs Civis & Aliados (Ferroforja e Arredores)

Estes prompts seguem o padrão de **"Miniatura Premium"** para completar o set visual da vila e das sidequests iniciais.

---

### 11. Mira Ferrosângue (Líder da Vila)

**Prompt:**

> Macro photography of a high-end, premium painted RPG miniature of 'Mira Ferrosângue'. A middle-aged human woman with a commanding presence, wearing practical leather and scale armor, holding a longsword and a small buckler. She looks heroic but weary. The miniature IS MOUNTED ON A DETAILED CIRCULAR BASE representing 'Ferroforja Village Gate': cobblestone floor with some rusty iron scraps and a small wooden post. Professional miniature painting style, sharp highlights. Solid, neutral light grey studio background. 45-degree isometric perspective.
