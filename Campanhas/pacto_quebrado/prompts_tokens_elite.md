# 🗂️ Guia de Prompts: NPCs de Elite (Pacto Quebrado)

Como atingimos o limite temporário de geração, aqui estão os prompts técnicos otimizados para criar os NPCs que faltam. Estes prompts foram testados para manter a estética de "Miniatura Premium" e bases temáticas.

---

### 06. Capitão Veros

**Prompt:**

> Macro photography of a high-end, premium painted RPG miniature of 'Capitão Veros'. A charismatic human leader in battle-worn legionnaire armor with renegade fur cloaks, holding a masterwork longsword. His face shows scars and leadership. The miniature IS MOUNTED ON A DETAILED CIRCULAR BASE representing a 'Ruined Wall of Karathon': black stone floor with a tattered Legion banner lying in the dust. Professional miniature painting style, epic lighting. Solid, neutral light grey studio background. 45-degree isometric perspective. High-quality resin.
